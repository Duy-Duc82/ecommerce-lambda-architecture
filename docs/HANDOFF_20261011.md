### Websosanh: key finding, the strongest candidate so far

Websosanh has already matched products across shops.

- Example: Samsung Galaxy A27 6/128 → **44 merchants**: thegioididong 8.490.000, cellphones 6.190.000 (used), fptshop 7.390.000, dienmayxanh 9.090.000, plus small shops.
- This answers cross-site product matching for free and gives price dispersion and anomaly material.
- Playwright rendering (`wss_render.py`, `inner_text("body")`) shows the offers: merchant domain, offer title, price, regions. There are 10 per page, paginated 1..5.
- The page gets them from XHR endpoints (`/compare-api/get-compare-verify-merchant`, `get-compare-normal-merchant?rootProductId=…&pageIndex=…`). Those are **internal APIs**, so per the advisor's rule, **parse the rendered DOM instead**.
- Discovery: robots.txt lists `product-sitemap.xml`; category pages are `/…/cat-NN.htm`.
- Not yet checked: rendered DOM selectors, pagination clicks, rate limits over many requests, sitemap size.

## Session 2 (2026-10-11 evening): done

Advisor report draft: `docs/evidence/source-hunt-20261011/bao-cao-nguon-du-lieu.html`. Scripts are in `scripts/`, results in `results/`.

**Websosanh correction.**
- robots.txt Disallows `/compare-api/*` and `/search-api/*`. A Playwright render still calls them, so the per-merchant list is out.
- Use only the JSON-LD `AggregateOffer` (lowPrice, highPrice, offerCount) on compare pages: 273,922 URLs in 25 product sitemaps. Also usable: the first 40 cards on category pages.
- Category card order changes on every load (Jaccard 0.09), so track a fixed panel of compare pages.

**Tiki product pages.**
- After about 25 product pages within a few minutes (2–3 s delay), Tiki serves a 17 KB obfuscated-JS challenge page.
- The block lifted after about 10–15 min.
- Category pages were unaffected during the block: 25/25 OK, 753 products. Tiki has 4,237 categories, so crawl by category.

**Pagination via URL.** Only Chợ Tốt works among the core six. Tiki, FPT, Phong Vũ, ĐMX, TGDD and Nguyễn Kim all repeat page 1.

**New sources.** A subagent probed 20 more sites. Good ones, all plain HTTP with URL pagination:

Excluded:

**Sitemap freshness.** TGDD scored 0/12 and CellphoneS 1/12 in-stock, so their sitemaps are mostly discontinued products.

**Chợ Tốt.** About 230 new phone listings per hour, about 1,200 per hour across all categories.

**Continuity.** 7 rounds of 10 sources and 4 rounds of 6 extra sources ran over about 1.5 h, all 100% success. Measured changes:

## Session 3 (2026-10-11, 19:40–20:25): deeper measurements

All results are in `docs/evidence/source-hunt-20261011/results/`. Raw `rounds*.jsonl` stayed in the scratchpad (ToS: no redistribution).

**Continuity.** 10 rounds for group A, plus 10 runs for group B and Lazada (3 runs). Totals: 144/145 loads OK. The one failure was a Bonbanh "máy chủ quá tải" page served with HTTP 200 (633 bytes); a reload 9 min later was fine.

**Price behaviour.**
- ĐMX rotates flash-sale prices: each TV goes up 9–37% for 30–45 min and then reverts. Needs ≤15 min cadence.
- Tiki: of 1,193 products in 34 categories, only 0.8% had `quantity_sold` rise in 31 min and 0.4% changed price, so a daily cadence fits.
- Pico `orderQuantity` rose within 10 min.

**Websosanh spread** (100 random compare pages): 96 live, median 8 offers; high/low > 3 for 22% and > 10 for 4%.

**Chợ Tốt.**
- 71–82% of listings are dealers (`company_ad`).
- IQR outliers per (model, capacity) are 1–4%, all suspiciously low prices.
- One price drop seen. A removed listing's page returns HTTP 410 Gone, which can serve as a time-to-sell label.

**Matching.**
- Laptops by manufacturer part code: 12/22 Phong Vũ laptops are on FPT, CellphoneS and Websosanh too.
- Naive slug matching for phones hits AppleCare+ pages, display units and wrong variants. Entity matching is needed; suggest adding it to the ML paper brief.

**Prototype.** `snapshot_adapter.py` maps all 16 sources to PRICE_SNAPSHOT plus validation rules (`sold_decreased`, `bad_price`, …). It produced a coverage matrix in the report.

**Tiki product pages.** No challenge page at a 15 s delay (24/24).

**No backfill source.** lichsugia.com and similar tools are lookup forms or third-party derived data, so no historical price data is available.
